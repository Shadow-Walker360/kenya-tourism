Welcome to Samburu

Welcome to Samburu National Reserve — a breathtaking stretch of savanna north of the equator, where Kenya shows off its wilder, quieter, and deeply spiritual side. The land is rugged, the skies are wide, and the people — the Samburu — still move with the rhythm of their ancestors.

The reserve’s lifeblood, the Ewaso Nyiro River, winds lazily through the arid land, giving life to palm groves and acacia woodlands that burst with elephants, lions, and rare species you’ll only find here.

Things to Do in Samburu

  • Wildlife Safari: Spot the Samburu Special Five — Grevy’s zebra, reticulated giraffe, Somali ostrich, beisa oryx, and gerenuk.
  • Cultural Visit: Meet the Samburu people, hear their songs, and learn their warrior traditions.
  • Ewaso Nyiro River Walk: A peaceful nature stroll along the river that gives life to this land.
  • Birdwatching: Watch lilac-breasted rollers, hornbills, and weaver birds light up the skies.
  • Star Gazing: The night skies in Samburu are crystal clear — galaxies feel within reach.

Hidden Gems of Samburu

💎 *The Singing Wells* — Where Samburu herders sing to their cattle as they draw water from the earth. 💎 *Lerata Hills* — A quiet spot perfect for sunrise meditation and storytelling. 💎 *Koitogor and Ololokwe Hills* — Sacred mountains that rise dramatically against the plains.
