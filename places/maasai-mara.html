About Maasai Mara

The Maasai Mara is Kenya's crown jewel where rolling savannas, endless herds of wildebeests, and lions rule the golden plains. Named after the Maasai people and the Mara River, this reserve offers an unparalleled safari experience.

What to Expect

  • Witness the Great Migration — over 1.5 million wildebeest crossing
  • Spot the Big Five: lions, elephants, buffalo, leopards, rhinos
  • Hot air balloon safari at sunrise over the plains
  • Visit traditional Maasai villages
  • Sundowner drinks watching African sunsets
