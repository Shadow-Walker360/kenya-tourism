Welcome to Tsavo

Welcome to **Tsavo National Park**, one of Kenya’s largest and oldest parks — a wild land split into **Tsavo East** and **Tsavo West**. Here, the earth burns red, the sunsets look like they’re painted by God Himself, and herds of elephants roam free, dusting themselves in the park’s signature ochre soil.

Tsavo isn’t just a safari — it’s an **experience that humbles your soul**. From volcanic hills and lava flows to palm-shaded oases, the park whispers the story of ancient Africa.

Things to Do in Tsavo

  • Game Drives: Witness the majestic elephants, lions, leopards, and rhinos in their natural glory.
  • Mzima Springs: Explore crystal-clear pools where hippos and crocodiles coexist under the volcanic shade.
  • Shetani Lava Flow: Hike through ancient black lava fields born from the Earth’s fiery rage.
  • Birdwatching: Over 500 bird species, from kingfishers to eagles, call this land home.
  • Safari Lodges: Stay in the heart of the wild — think candlelight dinners under acacia trees.
